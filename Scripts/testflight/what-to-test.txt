Duo Desk 1.0: control your Mac from iPhone Duo.

1. On the Mac, open Duo Workspace Host, start sharing and create an invitation (leave the address blank: it uses the Mac's Tailscale address).
2. In Duo Desk, choose Add Mac, paste the invitation, pair, and approve on the Mac if the six-digit codes match.
3. Try: taps and two-finger scrolls; pinch to zoom the picture (two-finger double-tap returns to fit); typing with the keyboard open while zoomed.
4. Try it on cellular over Tailscale and note how smooth it feels and whether the picture goes soft or stalls.
